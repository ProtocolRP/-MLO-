How to Customize the Logo in Redbrick Coffee House Map

To customize the logo in your map, follow these easy steps:

Open the "aura-coffehouse.ytd" file in your map resources.

Use OpenIV to open the "aura-coffehouse.ytd" file.
Enable "Edit Mode" in OpenIV.

Find "aura-logo.dds" inside "aura-coffehouse.ytd".

Right-click "aura-logo.dds" and select "Export".
Save it as a .dds file on your computer.

Open the .dds file in an image editor like Photoshop or GIMP.
Replace the photo with your own image and save it.
Your file must have an even number of pixels, so you can make it 512x512 pixels.

In OpenIV, right-click "aura-logo.dds" and select "Replace".
Choose your edited file.

Click "Save" in OpenIV.

Restart your server in FiveM to see the updated photo.
